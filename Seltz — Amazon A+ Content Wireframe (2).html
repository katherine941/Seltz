Seltz — Amazon A+ Content Wireframe

This wireframe maps out the seven A+ Content modules for every Seltz product on Amazon, across six product lines that each sell as a single tube and a six pack. It keeps the ingredients and comparison modules the brand already has, adds vibrant flavor colors to them so the page matches the updated gallery, adds a young brand image, a hydration message and a made in Germany message, and suggests a more elevated video to deliver later.

How to walk through it: pick a product, and scroll top to bottom the way a shopper would. Each card shows an example image (a third party layout reference only), a short visual description of our version, and the copy for that module. Red lines are required safety notes.
